# Ajeets Robots Product Category Glossary

Shared terminology for the Ajeets Robots Printify catalog. Use these terms consistently when discussing products with the team, in listings, marketing copy, and customer support.

---

## backpack

**Definition:** A two-strap bag worn on the back, designed for carrying books, laptops, clothing, or everyday essentials. Typically includes a main compartment, front pockets, and padded straps.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots || Classic Backpack

**When to use:** Use "backpack" when referring to any two-strap carrying bag worn on the back. Do not use for tote bags, duffels, or single-strap bags. Specify "laptop backpack" only if the product has a dedicated padded laptop sleeve (this one does).

---

## blanket

**Definition:** A large piece of soft fabric used as a covering for warmth, typically on a couch, bed, or during travel. Not worn as clothing.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robot - Sweatshirt Blanket

**When to use:** Use "blanket" or "throw blanket" for home textile coverings. Do not use "sweatshirt" alone — this product is a blanket made from sweatshirt-weight fabric, not a wearable sweatshirt. Always clarify "sweatshirt blanket" or "throw blanket" to avoid apparel confusion.

---

## clog footwear

**Definition:** A slip-on, closed-toe shoe with a rounded toe box and a thick, often foam or rubber sole. Designed for easy on/off wear, casual comfort, indoor/outdoor use.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Rounded Clog Style Footwear

**When to use:** Use "clogs" or "clog style footwear" for this slip-on casual shoe. Do not use "sneakers," "sandals," or "slippers" — clogs are a distinct category with a specific silhouette and use case.

---

## coasters

**Definition:** Small protective pads placed under drinkware to protect table surfaces from moisture rings, heat, and scratches. Usually sold in sets.

**Matching products:**
- Cork Coaster Set — Minimal "ajeets robots" Logo Drink Coasters (4-Pack)

**When to use:** Use "coasters" for drink surface protectors. Specify material when relevant — this set has an MDF top with a cork base. Do not confuse with "trivets" (larger, for hot pots/pans).

---

## hat / dad hat

**Definition:** A soft, unstructured 6-panel cap with a curved brim, adjustable strap, worn casually. The "dad hat" style is low-profile, relaxed fit, no structured crown.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Leather Patch Hat

**When to use:** Use "dad hat" or "cap" for this unstructured casual hat. Do not use "beanie," "snapback," "trucker hat," or "fitted cap" — those are different hat styles with different construction.

---

## hoodie / hooded sweatshirt

**Definition:** A midweight pullover sweatshirt with an attached hood, typically with a kangaroo pocket. Made from cotton-blend fleece, worn casually for warmth and comfort.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Hooded Sweatshirt
- Ajeets Robots - Techy Casual Streetwear

**When to use:** Use "hoodie" or "hooded sweatshirt" for pullover hooded tops. Do not use for crewneck sweatshirts (no hood) or zip-up hoodies unless the product specifically has a zipper. Specify "pullover hoodie" if zip-up variants exist in the catalog.

---

## journal

**Definition:** A bound blank book used for writing notes, thoughts, sketches, or daily entries. Smaller and more portable than a full notebook, typically for personal use.

**Matching products:**
- journal

**When to use:** Use "journal" for the 5" × 7.2" personal writing book. Use "notebook" for the larger, spiral-bound dotted notebook (see Notebook entry). Do not use interchangeably — journal implies a smaller, personal format.

---

## mousepad

**Definition:** A flat surface pad placed under a computer mouse to provide smooth tracking, protect the desk surface, and add ergonomic comfort.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Mousepad

**When to use:** Use "mousepad" for desk accessories designed for mouse tracking. This specific product is round — specify "round mousepad" to distinguish from standard rectangular mousepads if needed.

---

## mug

**Definition:** A handled ceramic drinking vessel, typically for hot beverages like coffee or tea. Usually 10–16 oz capacity.

**Matching products:**
- AJEETS ROBOTS - Classic Mug

**When to use:** Use "mug" for ceramic handled drinkware. This product is specifically a 12 oz latte mug with a C-handle. Do not confuse with "tumbler" (taller, often insulated, may be lidless or with a travel lid) or "water bottle" (sealed, portable).

---

## notebook

**Definition:** A bound collection of pages for writing, sketching, note-taking, or planning. Typically larger than a journal, often with specific page ruling.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Notebook

**When to use:** Use "notebook" for the spiral-bound dotted-page notebook. Use "journal" for the smaller 5" × 7.2" product. Specify "spiral notebook" or "dotted notebook" when the binding or page style matters.

---

## phone case

**Definition:** A protective cover that fits a specific smartphone model, protecting it from scratches, drops, and everyday wear while displaying a design.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Phone Case

**When to use:** Use "phone case" — always specify the compatible phone model (iPhone / Samsung Galaxy / Google Pixel + specific generation). Never say just "phone case" without confirming model compatibility. Available in Glossy or Matte finish — specify which when relevant.

---

## pillowcase

**Definition:** A removable fabric cover for a pillow, usually with a zipper or envelope closure. Protects the pillow and adds decorative style.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Square Pillowcase

**When to use:** Use "pillowcase" — not "pillow." This product is the cover only; no pillow insert is included. Specify "square pillowcase" and note that insert/fill is sold separately.

---

## polo

**Definition:** A short-sleeve collared knit shirt with a buttoned placket (typically 2–3 buttons). More formal than a t-shirt, less formal than a dress shirt. Also called a polo shirt.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robot's - Polo Tee

**When to use:** Use "polo" or "polo tee" for collared, button-placket shirts. This specific product is a youth/kids polo. Do not confuse with crew-neck tees — polos always have a collar and buttons.

---

## shower curtain

**Definition:** A water-resistant fabric or vinyl curtain hung in a shower/bathtub area to prevent water spray and provide privacy. Typically requires hooks/rings and a curtain rod (usually sold separately).

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Shower Curtain

**When to use:** Use "shower curtain" for bathroom privacy/water barrier curtains. Note: hooks are NOT included with this product. Specify dimensions (71" × 74") when discussing fit.

---

## sneakers

**Definition:** Athletic-style casual shoes with rubber soles, designed for walking and everyday wear. Lace-up closure, cushioned footbed.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Women's Sneakers

**When to use:** Use "sneakers" for athletic casual footwear with laces and rubber soles. Do not confuse with "clogs" (slip-on, no laces) — these are distinct footwear categories. Specify "women's sneakers" as this is a women-specific product.

---

## soft shell jacket

**Definition:** A lightweight, weather-resistant outer layer jacket made from stretch woven fabric with a bonded water-resistant membrane. Designed for mild weather, wind protection, and layering. Softer and more flexible than a hard shell.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Men's Soft Shell Jacket

**When to use:** Use "soft shell jacket" for this weather-resistant layering piece. Do not use "rain jacket," "windbreaker," or "fleece" — soft shell is a specific category combining wind/water resistance with breathability and stretch. Specify "men's" as this is a men-specific fit.

---

## streetwear

**Definition:** A casual fashion style originating from skate/surf/hip-hop culture, characterized by oversized fits, graphic prints, hoodies, and relaxed silhouettes. Emphasizes comfort and self-expression over formality.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Techy Casual Streetwear

**When to use:** Use "streetwear" as a style descriptor, not a product category. This specific product is a hoodie marketed in a streetwear aesthetic. Use the term when discussing brand positioning, styling, or target audience — not as a substitute for the actual product type (hoodie).

---

## sweatshirt

**Definition:** A casual, long-sleeve pullover top made from cotton-blend fleece, designed for warmth and comfort. Crewneck (no hood, no zipper). Thicker and warmer than a t-shirt, lighter than a jacket.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Hooded Sweatshirt
- Ajeets Robots - Women's Sweatshirt Full Size
- Ajeets Robots - Women's Sweatshirt Cropped
- Ajeets Robots - Women's Sweatshirt #1

**When to use:** Use "sweatshirt" or "crewneck sweatshirt" for pullover fleece tops without a hood. Use "hoodie" / "hooded sweatshirt" when the product has a hood. Specify "cropped sweatshirt" for the cropped variant. Do not use "sweatshirt" to refer to the Sweatshirt Blanket — that is a blanket, not apparel.

---

## tee / t-shirt

**Definition:** A short-sleeve, crew-neck casual shirt made from cotton or cotton-blend jersey knit. The most basic casual top, worn alone or layered.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robot's - Polo Tee (note: this is actually a polo, see Polo entry)
- Ajeets Robots - Kids Cotton Tee
- Ajeet Robot mens tee
- Ajeets Robots - Women's Tee

**When to use:** Use "tee" or "t-shirt" for crew-neck, short-sleeve knit tops. Do NOT use for polo shirts (those have collars and buttons — use "polo" instead), sweatshirts, or hoodies. Always specify the audience when relevant: "kids tee," "men's tee," "women's tee."

**Note:** "Ajeets Robot's - Polo Tee" appears in searches for "tee" but is actually a polo shirt — use the Polo entry for that product.

---

## tote

**Definition:** A large, open-top carrying bag with two parallel shoulder straps/handles. Typically unlined, used for shopping, beach trips, or everyday carry. Emphasizes capacity and casual style over structure.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Woven Straw Tote

**When to use:** Use "tote" or "straw tote" for this woven carryall bag. Do not confuse with "backpack" (two shoulder straps, worn on back, zippered compartments) — totes are open-top shoulder bags. Specify "woven straw tote" to convey material and style.

---

## tumbler

**Definition:** A tall, insulated drinking cup, typically stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation, designed to keep beverages hot or cold for extended periods. May include a lid.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Tumbler

**When to use:** Use "tumbler" for the 20 oz insulated stainless steel drink cup. Do not confuse with "mug" (ceramic, usually no lid, for hot drinks at home) or "water bottle" (sealed, portable, usually narrower). Tumblers are the in-between: insulated like a bottle, open-top like a mug, with optional lid.

---

## wall art

**Definition:** Decorative artwork designed to be hung on a wall. Includes framed prints, canvas prints, posters, and similar flat decorative pieces.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Canvas Wall Art Print

**When to use:** Use "wall art" or "canvas wall art" for decorative prints meant to hang on walls. Specify "canvas wall art" / "canvas print" for this product — it's printed on artist-grade cotton canvas, hand-stretched over a wood frame, ready to hang. Do not confuse with posters (paper, unframed) or other wall decor.

---

## wall calendar

**Definition:** A 12-month calendar designed to hang on a wall, typically with one month per page, used for tracking dates, appointments, and events throughout the year.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - 2026 Wall Calendar

**When to use:** Use "wall calendar" for the yearly date-tracking product. Specify the year ("2026 Wall Calendar") to avoid confusion with other years. Do not confuse with "planner" or "journal" — a wall calendar is a large-format, wall-mounted date reference, not a personal writing book.

---

## water bottle

**Definition:** A portable, sealable container designed for carrying drinking water. Typically reusable, with a screw cap or sport lid, sized to fit in bags and cup holders.

**Matching products:**
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Water Bottle

**When to use:** Use "water bottle" for portable, sealed drinking containers. Do not confuse with "tumbler" (often open-top or with a sipping lid, less portable) or "mug" (ceramic, for home use). Specify capacity (20 oz) when discussing fit/portability.
